### CHAI-PAANI
**Rules text (verbatim):** "Pick an opponent and pick a resource. Whenever they spend that resource, it comes to you instead of going to the Public Reserve. If another such card is played, this one will get discarded. (1/2)"

**Bullets:**
- ▶ There are two such cards in the deck. This one remains in play until the second one is played. When the second 'Chai-Paani' card is played, this one will be deactivated and discarded.
- ▶ This card only leeches resources spent on voters and power cards. Resources that are traded with other players or the bank are not leeched.
- ▶ A second 'Chai-Paani' card can be applied on the same player. The first one will get deactivated.

### JUMLA
**Rules text (verbatim):** "This is an extra Ideology Card of your choice. Opponents can take this card by paying you as many resources as the Ideologue level this card is on. At the end of your turn, you may place this card under a different Ideologue."

**Bullets:**

- ▶ You may place it under a different Ideologue but only at the end of your turn.
